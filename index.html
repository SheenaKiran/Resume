01 — PROFILE

Academic Profile

Dr. Sheena N. is an Assistant Professor and Head of the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at the College of Engineering Aranmula, with over two decades of teaching experience across engineering colleges in Kerala. Her doctoral work at Cochin University of Science & Technology focused on the design of energy-efficient IoT network protocols with improved security, privacy, and lifetime.

Alongside this core research in wireless and IoT systems, she works actively in machine learning, deep learning, data analytics, and natural language processing.

She regularly leads hands-on workshops and faculty development programs on these topics — from deep learning and computer vision to Power BI dashboards and REST API deployment for ML models — across colleges in Kerala, and reviews for journals including the Journal of Supercomputing and Scientific Reports. She has published many papers in peer-reviewed journals and international conferences, and has also published a book. As an academician, she has served in academic leadership roles including Dean of Academic Affairs and additional charge of Principal, led her department as Head on multiple occasions, and coordinated KTU and postgraduate affairs — a career shaped as much by strengthening the systems around teaching and research as by the research itself.
